⛏️ How to Mine

🎯 Objective

Clear the minefield without detonating any bombs. Reveal all safe cells to win!

🖱️ Controls

  • Left-click — Reveal a cell
  • Right-click — Cycle: 👁️ Reveal Box Content

🎮 Keyboard Shortcuts

  • R — Restart game
  • F — Toggle reveal mode (click to safely reveal a cell)
  • 1 / 2 / 3 — Switch difficulty
  • ? — Toggle this help

💎 Scoring

  • Revealing a number adds its value to your score
  • Chording bonus: +5 points for each chording reveal
  • Speed bonus: Time-based multiplier for winning fast
  • Difficulties: Medium score ×1.5, Hard score ×2

📊 Tips

  • Numbers tell you how many bombs are adjacent
  • Use flags to mark suspected bombs
  • Question marks help track uncertain cells
  • First click is always safe!
